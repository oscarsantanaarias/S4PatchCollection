fix_c03_arcade_overflow.cpp

Bug: CArcadeStream_ReadBytes_nodstcap at 0x01155F70 copies a P2P entry into
a buffer using a length the other player controls. It only checks the
source has enough bytes, never the destination size. The caller,
CArcadeStream_ParseEntries at 0x014E0DD0, passes it a char[256] on the
stack. Send an entry over 256 bytes and it overflows that buffer.

Confirmed with two accounts. Inflated an outgoing entry to 300 bytes on
one client, caught the oversized read on the other - size 300, caller
inside ParseEntries.

Only actually fired in Warfare, not Arcade. Hooked the write side at
0x01155FC0 too, it sat idle through several arcade actions, then caught
a real entry going out during a Warfare match, len 44 to 300. Primitive
isn't arcade-only despite the name, just didn't get exercised from the
arcade UI in testing.

Fix: hook 0x01155F70, clamp size to 255 if it comes in over 256. Always
on, no flag, no logging.
